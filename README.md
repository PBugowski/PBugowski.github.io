## Projects
- ### NYC Accidents 2020
The project analyzes road incidents in New York City in 2020, focusing on locations, timing, vehicle types, and injuries or fatalities.  
It identifies areas and situations where accident frequency is linked to more serious outcomes, with particular attention to pedestrians and cyclists.  
The analysis accounts for incomplete location data and the unusual traffic conditions during the COVID-19 pandemic.  
The goal is to identify priority locations for further investigation and field observation.

- ### KajoDataSpace Transactions Analysis for 2023 - 2026
The project analyzes customer transaction activity from 2023–2026, focusing on purchase frequency, returning customers, and payment patterns.  
It examines whether changes in total payments are driven by transaction volume or transaction value, as well as customer return intervals.  
The analysis distinguishes observed patterns from possible explanations given the limited data available.  
The goal is to provide a clear view of customer activity and identify additional data needed for deeper analysis.

[PowerBI](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/11/4240) / [Sql](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/8/3048)

- ### Toys Distribution in Mexico
The project analyzes toy sales in Mexico to identify products and stores worth discussing with the purchasing team.  
It examines sales patterns by product, category, city, store type, seasonality, and inventory levels.  
The analysis highlights potential supply priorities while accounting for limitations such as missing stock-out history and estimated historical margins.  
The goal is to distinguish products that sell quickly from those that remain in inventory too long.

[PowerBI](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/11/4240) / [Sql](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/8/3048)

- ### Lego 1970 - 2022
The project explores how LEGO sets have changed over time, using data on release years, themes, piece counts, minifigures, age ranges, and list prices.  
It looks for trends in set size, pricing, themes, and differences between age groups.  
The analysis also considers limitations such as incomplete historical records and changes in the purchasing power of the dollar.  
The goal is to create an engaging data-driven story that connects LEGO sets across different eras.

[Sql](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/8/3048)

- ### S&P500 for 2014 - 2017
The project analyzes daily stock data from 2014–2017, focusing on price movements, volatility, and trading volume.  
The goal is to identify unusual trading sessions and relationships between price changes and market activity.  
The analysis also considers data limitations, including the lack of adjustments for stock splits and dividends and the absence of news context.  
The final outcome is a short, data-driven story about selected companies and trading sessions worth exploring.

[Sql](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/8/3048)

- ### Supersales
The project builds a unified view of the customer order journey at Supersales, connecting customers, products, orders, shipping, ratings, returns, and cost forecasts.  
It explores repeat customers, product combinations, delivery times, and how these factors relate to customer ratings.  
The analysis highlights data limitations, including missing ratings, returns, and historical price changes.  
The goal is to create a cross-functional story showing how the shopping basket, delivery, and customer experience are connected.

[Sql](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/8/3048)

- ### UK Rail for 01.2024 - 04.2024
The project analyzes passenger journeys on the UK rail network, focusing on delays, disruptions, and refund requests.  
It compares routes, travel times, ticket types, and disruption causes to identify recurring patterns.  
The analysis distinguishes refund requests from confirmed payouts and considers journey status and data limitations.  
The goal is to identify routes and issues that deserve closer attention from customer service and operations teams.

[MS Excel](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/8/3048)

- ### Video Games Sales for 1971 - 2024
The project explores how video game popularity varies across regions, platforms, genres, and critical reception.  
It identifies less obvious success stories, including highly rated games with low sales and commercially popular titles with weaker reviews.  
The analysis compares regional performance while accounting for missing data and unclear sales units.  
The goal is to select compelling games for a data-driven story and identify questions for further research.

[MS Excel](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/8/3048)

- ### INC 5000 List for 2019
The project analyzes companies from the 2019 Inc. 5000 list, looking for less obvious stories of rapid growth.  
It compares their industries, locations, revenues, employee numbers, founding years, and appearances on the list.  
The goal is to identify interesting business clusters and companies worth exploring in greater depth.
